AI Engineer — Latin America (remote)

We're hiring an AI Engineer to design and build AI-powered products that
improve how the heavy-equipment industry accesses service knowledge and
parts information. You'll lead the development of intelligent retrieval
systems, agentic architectures, and AI solutions that capture field repairs
and generate verified training content while balancing advanced AI
techniques with production reliability.

Responsibilities
- Design and implement intelligent retrieval systems that help technicians
  quickly find procedures, parts, and troubleshooting guidance across
  multiple service data sources.
- Build and maintain RAG pipelines, graph-based retrieval approaches, and
  agentic architectures to answer complex diagnostic questions across
  structured and unstructured data.
- Architect and deploy production-grade AI systems in cloud environments
  (GCP and AWS), integrating advanced AI models with proprietary data and
  scaling retrieval workloads reliably.
- Design APIs and controlled data-access patterns that enable customers to
  build AI solutions using proprietary data while managing hallucination
  risk and enforcing scope controls.
- Collaborate cross-functionally with product, engineering, and customer
  success teams to translate user needs into scalable AI capabilities.

Requirements
- 5+ years of experience working as a software engineer.
- 2+ years building production AI/ML systems, including retrieval-based
  architectures such as RAG pipelines, agentic systems, or tool-calling
  workflows.
- 5+ years of professional Python and building production-grade systems
  using clean architecture and engineering best practices.
- 2+ years deploying and scaling AI workloads on GCP or AWS.
- Strong understanding of embedding strategies, vector databases, and
  retrieval techniques (single-step, multi-step, graph-based / GraphRAG).
- Hands-on experience with LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini), prompt
  engineering, and multimodal models.
- Demonstrated ability to design and evaluate high-accuracy AI systems
  including retrieval-quality measurement, validation strategies, and
  graceful failure mechanisms.

Bonus
- BSc in Computer Science or related field.
